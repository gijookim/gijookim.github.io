## Course Role

Co-Instructor (with Dr. Y.-O. Kim), Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, Seoul National University.

## Course Description

This course introduces current challenges in water resources systems planning and management, deterministic and stochastic optimization algorithms, and frontier problems in the field. Students actively use laptop-based coding exercises throughout the course, and the final project asks them to apply course materials to real-world water resources engineering problems.
