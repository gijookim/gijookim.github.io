## Course Role

Instructor, Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering, Seoul National University.

## Course Description

This course introduces methods for analyzing uncertainty and variability in hydrologic processes, probabilistic modeling, extreme event analysis, regression models, and time series models. Students actively use laptop-based coding exercises throughout the course, and the final project asks them to apply course materials to real-world hydrological data.
